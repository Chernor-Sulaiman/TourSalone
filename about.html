About TourSalone

Promoting Sierra Leone Beauty to the World

Discover Our Country

TourSalone is a tourism information website created to help people discover beautiful and interesting places in Sierra Leone.

The website brings together information about beaches, wildlife, history, culture and adventure.

It is designed for local visitors, international tourists, students and anyone interested in exploring Sierra Leone.
